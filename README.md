# Polaroid Wall

Polaroid Wall is a short, interactive p5.js sketch created as a companion piece to Ariel Sol's multimedia brass band composition "Thirty Crossed Paths: A Polaroid Wall from a City in Transit." 

Thirty Crossed Paths is a collection of moments from my first year in Los Angeles, arranged to romanticize the mixed bag of experiences I felt exploring the new city. This sketch pairs musical movements with photos taken during the time of writing, color-coded with strings representing unexplained connections across time and place.
